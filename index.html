    Platinum Credit Limited is a credit only Micro-Finance Company licensed in Kenya under the Company’s Act. The company, was founded in 2003 with a vision to provide emergency loans to Civil Servants, TSC members and employees of selected companies. We deliver cash in 24hours, with minimum bureaucracy and swift customer service.

     

    Sales and Marketing

    • Job Type Full Time , Onsite
    • Qualification Diploma , First School Leaving Certificate , KCSE , Others , Postgraduate Diploma , Professional Certificate , Vocational
    • Experience
    • Location Nairobi
    • Job Field Sales and Business Development 
    • Salary Range KSh 16,000 - KSh 30,000/month
    • The Sales & Marketing Officer will be responsible for identifying and generating new business opportunities, prospecting for potential clients, promoting and selling the company’s products and services, conducting field and digital marketing activities, building and maintaining strong client relationships, following up on leads and prospects, converting opportunities into sales, and achieving set monthly targets. The role requires consistent market outreach, client engagement, lead generation, pipeline management, and timely reporting of sales activities and performance.
    • This is a commission-based position with earnings directly linked to sales performance, making it ideal for a self-motivated, target-driven individual who is passionate about sales and willing to work in a fast-paced, performance-driven environment.This position is opened in our Westlands Branch.
